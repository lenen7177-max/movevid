Duo Dance turns two photos into a vertical dance video: two dancers, one synced routine, side by side in a bright studio. This guide walks through what the template does, which photos to pick and how to get the finished video onto TikTok.

## What the Duo Dance trend is

Duo dance clips are the side-by-side routines friends film together: an entrance, a few synced counts, a close-up and a shared final pose. The fun is in the matching moves and the chemistry between the two dancers. This template recreates that format from two photos, so nobody has to rehearse or film anything.

## Photo tips

- **One person per photo.** The first photo plays the left dancer, the second the right dancer.
- **A clear, front-facing face.** Faces turned away or covered by hair, sunglasses or a hand come out less like the person.
- **Even light.** Soft daylight works better than harsh shadows or strong backlight.
- **JPG, PNG or WebP, under 20 MB.** Larger, sharper photos keep more detail.
- **Only people who agreed.** Use photos of yourself or adults who are happy to be in the video. Photos of celebrities or public figures don't pass the photo review.

## Step by step

1. **Upload two photos.** Open the Duo Dance template and add one photo to each slot (left dancer, right dancer). No photos to hand? "Try sample photos" shows a finished example for free when the site has one.
2. **Pick a version and quality.** Fast makes the highlight cut; Premium makes the full video. The price in credits is shown on each option before anything renders:

On a computer the TikTok button downloads the MP4, copies a caption and opens TikTok's upload page, so you drag the video in and paste the caption. If TikTok mutes the music, add a track from its sound library.

## Common mistakes

- **Group photos.** Two people in one photo confuse the dancer it should play. Crop each photo to one person.
- **Expecting to choose the moves.** The routine comes from the template, so every Duo Dance video follows the same choreography. That is what makes it recognizable as the trend.
- **Picking Fast for the full routine.** Fast renders the highlight cut. Pick Premium for the whole <TemplateValue template="duo_dance" name="seconds" />-second video.
- **Using photos of other people without asking.** Everyone in the video has to agree to be in it, and photos of public figures are rejected.
