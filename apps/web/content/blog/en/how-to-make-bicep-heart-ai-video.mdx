## What the bicep heart trend is

The bicep heart is a pose trend that took off on TikTok in late October 2026 under #bicepheart. You flex one arm and curl your other hand against the inside of the bicep, so the curve of the arm and the hand make one heart. It's a heart sign with a flex: playful, a little proud, and easy to send to someone.

## Photo tips

- **One person, head to waist, both arms in view.** The pose needs both arms, so a close-up selfie works less well.
- **A clear, front-facing face.** Faces turned away or covered by hair, sunglasses or a hand come out less like you.
- **Even light.** Soft daylight works better than harsh shadows or strong backlight.
- **JPG, PNG or WebP, under 20 MB.** Larger, sharper photos keep more detail.
- **Only people who agreed.** Use your own photo or a friend's who said yes. Photos of celebrities or public figures don't pass the photo review.

## Common mistakes

- **Arms cropped out.** If the photo stops at the shoulders, the arms have to be guessed. Use a photo that shows both arms.
- **Group photos.** Several people in one photo make it unclear who does the pose. Crop the photo to one person.
- **Posting it as real.** Keep TikTok's AI-generated label on.
